## Track Structure

- Two tracks total. Every team starts in **Track 1: Lifestyle & Personal Productivity**, which has two problem statements (teams pick one — we picked **Stress & Workload Manager**, see `02-problem-statement.md`).
- After prototype judging, the **top 10 Track 1 teams** become finalists and go on to build/deploy a real app.
- Teams *not* selected may optionally join **Track 2: Industry** (low-code/no-code, sponsor-backed, revealed ~21 Sept) — but a Track 1 finalist cannot also join Track 2 unless they voluntarily give up their Track 1 spot.
- 10 finalists per track (20 total), 3 winners per track (6 total).
- 200+ teams competing overall; only 18 mentors available across all of them.

## Mentorship

- Runs 31 Aug – 13 Sep 2026, fully virtual on Discord.
- Sessions are booked from a shared Excel sheet, **first-come-first-served**, max 25 minutes each, **max one booking per day**.
- Mentors are not assigned to teams — pick whoever matches the domain we need help with (AI, web, mobile, UX, pitching, etc.).
- Mentors won't design the solution or write code for us — they question assumptions and point out best practices. The idea stays ours.
- **Skipping mentorship entirely costs 7% of the Ideation score** (see rubric). At least one session is worth doing.
- After every session: log the date, mentor, feedback received, and what we changed because of it (this feeds directly into the submission — see `04-submission-requirements.md`).

## Submission Logistics

- **Deadline: 13 Sept 2026, 11:59 PM.** Submit via Google Form (team leader submits once per team). Aim to submit 15 minutes early — late submissions are not accepted.
- Submission = a **public GitHub repo** whose `README.md` links to everything: project overview, ideation/process assets, design/prototype links, and the video.
- Video: 3–5 minutes, uploaded to YouTube, set to **unlisted**, named with the team name.
- All linked design tools (Figma/Canva/etc.) must be set to public/viewable — test in an incognito window.
